Privacy

A preview with minimal data collection.

This preview does not enable analytics or advertising.

Favorites and recent vehicles

When you save or view a vehicle, the page can store its name and URL in your browser's local storage. Recall follow-up checkboxes are also stored on this device, keyed only to the displayed vehicle and recall campaign. That information stays in this browser and is not transmitted to Vehicle Intelligence. Clear it using your browser's site-data controls.

VIN recall lookup

VIN entry is used only in the open page. When you choose to continue, the page copies the VIN to your clipboard if your browser allows it and opens NHTSA's official recall lookup. Paste the VIN into NHTSA's page yourself. Vehicle Intelligence does not submit the VIN to its own server or save it. NHTSA's site is governed by its own privacy practices.

Hosting

The hosting provider may process standard request information needed to deliver the site, such as an IP address and user agent, under its own policies. Vehicle Intelligence does not add tracking scripts in this preview.

Future changes

If data practices change, this notice will be updated before related features are enabled.
