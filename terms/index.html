Terms and safety notice

Use the evidence as a starting point.

Vehicle Intelligence provides general information from public records. It is not professional, mechanical, legal, or safety advice.

Vehicle-specific decisions

Public model-year records do not determine the condition, recall status, repair history, or suitability of a particular vehicle. Check recalls by VIN with NHTSA and consult a qualified professional before purchase, repair, or safety decisions.

Data limitations

Sources may be incomplete, delayed, revised, or unavailable. Complaint reports are not necessarily verified and are not adjusted for vehicle population or exposure. We provide provenance and retrieval dates so readers can inspect current official records.

Independence

Vehicle Intelligence is not affiliated with NHTSA or vehicle manufacturers. Product and organization names belong to their respective owners.
