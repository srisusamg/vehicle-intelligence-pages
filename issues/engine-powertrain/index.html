How to interpret this topic

Vehicle Intelligence maps official NHTSA component labels into a stable issue taxonomy. A complaint can mention several components and can therefore contribute to more than one topic. This share describes composition within complaint reports; it does not compare the vehicle with sales, mileage, or another model.
