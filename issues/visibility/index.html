How to interpret this topic

Vehicle Intelligence maps official NHTSA component labels into a stable issue taxonomy. A complaint can mention several components and can therefore contribute to more than one topic. This share describes composition within complaint reports; it does not compare vehicles using sales, mileage, age, or another exposure denominator.

The vehicles shown here passed the same source and quality checks, but their reporting windows and populations can differ. Do not use raw report counts as a ranking or prediction about an individual vehicle.
