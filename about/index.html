Trust through restraint

We make public vehicle records easier to read.

Vehicle Intelligence is an independently operated consumer information project. It organizes official public data while preserving source context and uncertainty.

Who operates this site

The named operator and publisher is the Vehicle Intelligence project. It is independent of NHTSA, the United States government, vehicle manufacturers, dealers, insurers, and advertising networks.

What we do

We retrieve first-party records, validate their structure and relationships, group technical labels into inspectable topics, and link summaries back to their sources.

What we do not do

We do not turn complaint counts into reliability ratings, claim that reports are verified failure events, or predict whether a specific vehicle will have a problem. We do not accept payment to alter the evidence.

Corrections and questions

Report a correction or source discrepancy through the public Vehicle Intelligence issue tracker ↗. Do not include VINs, addresses, phone numbers, or other personal information. The recent updates page records material changes; the methodology explains calculations and limitations.
