Official vehicle-specific lookup

Check a vehicle for open recalls

Model-year recall lists are useful context. NHTSA’s VIN lookup is the place to check whether an unrepaired recall is associated with your specific vehicle.

Vehicle specific

Check open recalls for your VIN

NHTSA checks whether an unrepaired safety recall is associated with a specific vehicle. We do not send or save your VIN.

Your VIN stays in this page until you choose to copy it. Paste it into NHTSA’s official lookup.

NHTSA’s VIN lookup reports unrepaired recalls it can match. Some recent, older, completed, or limited-manufacturer recalls may not appear. Confirm current applicability with NHTSA or the manufacturer.

i

What the lookup can and cannot confirm

NHTSA’s results cover unrepaired recalls it can match to your VIN. They may omit some recent recalls while vehicle identification is underway, completed recalls, recalls more than 15 years old, some limited-manufacturer recalls, and international vehicles. Contact the manufacturer or dealer for confirmation and current service instructions.
