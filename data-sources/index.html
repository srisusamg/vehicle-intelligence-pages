Data sources

The current preview uses four official NHTSA interfaces. The latest vehicle snapshot was refreshed September 13, 2026.

NHTSA complaints

The Office of Defects Investigation complaint database contains public reports submitted by consumers and others. NHTSA lists daily update frequency. Important fields include ODI number, vehicle identity, filed and incident dates, component labels, and crash or fire flags. Durable public summaries omit VINs and complaint narratives.

NHTSA recalls

Recall campaign records include campaign number, manufacturer, component, received date, summary, consequence, and remedy. Consumers must use a VIN lookup to determine vehicle-specific applicability and completion.

Investigations

The official investigations flat file records defect-assessment and investigation actions. Exact model-year selection uses action number plus component as row identity so distinct component rows are not collapsed.

Manufacturer communications

The official manufacturer-communications archive includes service bulletins, repair instructions, and other manufacturer documents. A document can repeat across product and component rows, so matching document records are deduplicated only when their identifiers and document-level fields agree.

Usage and limitations

These are United States government public-data interfaces. Availability and schemas can change; the pipeline validates expected structures and stops on mismatches. Requests use caching and bounded retries. Every evidence brief attributes and links its exact sources.
